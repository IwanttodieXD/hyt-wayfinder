# ☕ Barista Mini-Game - Complete Gameplay Guide

## 🎯 Game Objective

Master the art of coffee making by adding precise measurements of coffee, water, and milk. Score 90+ to earn TESDA certification!

---

## 🎮 Step-by-Step Gameplay

### Starting the Game

1. **Enter the 3D Tour** from the homepage
2. **Click anywhere** to lock your pointer
3. **Navigate** to Ground Floor, Left Wing
4. **Find** the brown counter with coffee equipment
5. **Click the green glowing button** on the grinder to start

---

### 🫘 Step 1: Grinding Coffee

**Target**: 18 grams of coffee grounds

#### What You'll See
- Title: "🫘 Grinding Coffee"
- Instruction: "Add 18g of coffee grounds"
- Progress bar showing current grams vs. target
- Visual: Coffee grounds filling up in the grinder

#### Controls
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Current: 12.5g    Target: 18g     │
│  ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░░░░░              │
│         [HOLD to Add] [Next Step]   │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### How to Play
1. **Click and HOLD** the "HOLD to Add" button
2. Watch the blue bar fill up
3. **Release** when you reach 17.5-18.5g (green zone)
4. Click "Next Step" when satisfied

#### Scoring
- **18.0g exactly**: 100 points ✓ PERFECT!
- **17.5-18.5g**: 90 points ✓ Great!
- **17-19g**: 75 points ○ Good
- **16-20g**: 50 points ⚠️ Okay
- **Below/Above**: 20 points ✗ Poor

#### Tips
💡 The coffee adds at **0.5g per 50ms**
💡 Small taps = more control
💡 Aim slightly under - easier to add than remove!

## 📊 Understanding the Progress Bar

### Visual Guide
```
Target Zone (Perfect: 98-102%)
        ↓
[███████████████▓▓░░░░░░░░░]
 ←Blue Fill→ ▲    ↑
            Perfect   Red = Overflow!
```

### Color Meanings
- 🔵 **Blue**: Still adding, below target
- 🟢 **Green**: Perfect range (98-102%)
- 🟡 **Yellow**: Good range (90-110%)
- 🔴 **Red**: Overflow! (over 110%)

### Status Messages
- "Keep adding..." - You're below target
- "○ Good" - You're in acceptable range
- "✓ PERFECT!" - You're in the green zone
- "⚠️ OVERFLOW! Release now!" - Stop immediately!

## 🎯 Pro Tips for High Scores

### General Strategy
1. **Start slow** - Get a feel for the speed
2. **Watch the bar** more than the numbers
3. **Release early** - You can always add more
4. **Practice each step** - Learn the speeds

### Speed Reference
- Coffee: **Slowest** (0.5g per tick) - Easiest control
- Water: **Medium** (1ml per tick) - Moderate control  
- Milk: **Fastest** (3ml per tick) - Hardest to control!

### Advanced Techniques
1. **Pulse holding**: Tap and release repeatedly for fine control
2. **Anticipate**: Release ~1-2% before target
3. **Visual cues**: Watch the 3D containers filling
4. **Green zone**: Aim for center of green zone, not edges

### Common Mistakes
❌ Holding too long on milk (most common!)
❌ Not watching the overflow warning
❌ Clicking "Next Step" too early
❌ Panicking and releasing too late

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### "I can't find the cafe"
- Stay on ground floor
- Look left (west) when you spawn
- Walk forward with W key
- Counter is brown with silver equipment

### "The bar fills too fast"
- That's normal for milk (fastest)
- Use short taps instead of holding
- Practice the timing

### "I keep overflowing"
- Watch for the overflow warning
- Release 1-2% before target
- Start with coffee (slowest) to practice

### "Score seems wrong"
- Check each step's accuracy
- Overflow penalties are harsh
- Aim for green zone on all steps
